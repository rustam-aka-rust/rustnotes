### **Five Job Advertisements for Language Major Students**

These **fictional job ads** are crafted to align with **students studying languages**, helping them engage with **realistic job opportunities** related to their field.

---

### **1. English Language Tutor (Freelance)**
📍 **Location:** Remote (Online)  
💰 **Salary:** $15 - $25 per hour  
📅 **Job Type:** Part-Time, Freelance  

**Job Description:**  
Do you have a passion for teaching English and helping students improve their communication skills? We are looking for **enthusiastic and patient tutors** to teach English to non-native speakers. Lessons will focus on **speaking, writing, and exam preparation**.

**Responsibilities:**  
✔ Conduct **one-on-one or small group lessons** online.  
✔ Create **customized lesson plans** based on student needs.  
✔ Provide **constructive feedback** on speaking & writing skills.  

**Requirements:**  
✅ Native or near-native **English proficiency**.  
✅ Experience in **language teaching or tutoring** is a plus.  
✅ Strong **interpersonal and communication skills**.  

📝 *Apply by submitting your CV and a 5-minute demo lesson recording!*  

---

### **2. Bilingual Content Writer (English & Spanish)**
📍 **Location:** Madrid, Spain (Hybrid)  
💰 **Salary:** €30,000 - €35,000 per year  
📅 **Job Type:** Full-Time  

**Job Description:**  
Join our **fast-growing media company** as a **Bilingual Content Writer**! If you love writing and can switch seamlessly between **English and Spanish**, this is the job for you.

**Responsibilities:**  
✔ Write **engaging blog articles** on travel, business, and culture.  
✔ Translate and adapt **marketing materials** for global audiences.  
✔ Collaborate with editors and social media managers.  

**Requirements:**  
✅ Bachelor’s degree in **Languages, Journalism, or Communication**.  
✅ Fluent in **English and Spanish** (written & spoken).  
✅ Strong **research and storytelling skills**.  

📝 *Send us your CV and two writing samples (one in each language)!*

### **3. Localization Specialist (Gaming Industry)**
📍 **Location:** Tokyo, Japan  
💰 **Salary:** ¥4,000,000 - ¥6,000,000 per year  
📅 **Job Type:** Full-Time  

**Job Description:**  
Are you a **language enthusiast and gamer**? Join our **localization team** to ensure **our video games feel natural and immersive in English**.

**Responsibilities:**  
✔ Translate **dialogues, menus, and in-game text** from Japanese to English.  
✔ Adapt cultural references to fit Western audiences.  
✔ Work closely with game developers & voice actors.  

**Requirements:**  
✅ **Advanced Japanese & native-level English**.  
✅ Background in **Translation, Linguistics, or Gaming**.  
✅ Passion for video games & understanding of **gaming terminology**.  

🎮 *Bonus points if you’ve worked on game translations before!*

### **5. Junior Interpreter for Business Conferences**
📍 **Location:** Paris, France (On-Site)  
💰 **Salary:** €35,000 per year + travel benefits  
📅 **Job Type:** Contract (6 months)  

**Job Description:**  
We are looking for a **Junior Interpreter** to assist at **international business events** in Paris. If you love working in **multicultural environments** and have a **strong command of business language**, apply today!

**Responsibilities:**  
✔ Interpret **live business discussions** between English & French speakers.  
✔ Assist executives in **meetings & negotiations**.  
✔ Translate **business reports and emails** when needed.  

**Requirements:**  
✅ Degree in **Translation, Interpreting, or Business Communication**.  
✅ Fluent in **English & French** (spoken & written).  
✅ Ability to work in **high-pressure situations**.  

🎤 *Bonus: Experience with simultaneous interpreting is a plus!*

### **How to Use These Job Ads in Class**
📌 **Activity 1: Job Ad Analysis** – Students identify **hidden meanings** in the job descriptions (e.g., *What does "fast-paced environment" really mean?*).  
📌 **Activity 2: Cover Letter Writing** – Students **write an application** for their preferred job.  
📌 **Activity 3: Mock Interviews** – One student acts as **the hiring manager**, and another as **the applicant**.  

These ads **simulate real-world job applications** and allow students to apply **language and communication skills** to **career-focused scenarios**. Would you like any additional customization? 🚀
