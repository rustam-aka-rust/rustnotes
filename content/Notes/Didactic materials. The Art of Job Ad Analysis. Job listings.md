## Didactic materials. The Art of Job Ad Analysis. Job listings

### **1. Marketing Coordinator**

*Job Description:*

Join our dynamic team as a Marketing Coordinator! We're seeking a self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced environment. The ideal candidate will be a team player with excellent communication skills and a passion for innovative marketing strategies. Responsibilities include managing multiple projects simultaneously, developing creative content, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to drive brand awareness.

*Phrases to Analyze:*

- Dynamic team
- Self-starter
- Fast-paced environment
- Team player
- Excellent communication skills
- Managing multiple projects simultaneously

### **2. Sales Associate**

*Job Description:*

We are looking for a results-driven Sales Associate to join our family-like team. The successful candidate will have a go-getter attitude and be comfortable with ambiguity. Key responsibilities include building strong customer relationships, meeting sales targets, and contributing to a dynamic work environment.

*Phrases to Analyze:*

- Family-like team
- Go-getter attitude
- Comfortable with ambiguity
- Dynamic work environment

### **3. Administrative Assistant**

*Job Description:*

Our company is seeking an Administrative Assistant with great attention to detail and a proactive mindset. The ideal candidate will be a jack-of-all-trades, capable of multitasking and handling various administrative duties in our fast-paced office. Responsibilities include managing calendars, coordinating meetings, and assisting with special projects as needed.

*Phrases to Analyze:*

- Great attention to detail
- Proactive mindset
- Jack-of-all-trades
- Multitasking
- Fast-paced office

**Activity:**

In pairs, students can review these job descriptions and identify the ambiguous or clichéd phrases. They can then discuss the potential underlying meanings or implications of these terms, referencing insights from the article [[Lesson plan -- The Art of Job Ad Analysis|"The Art of Job Ad Analysis: The Real Message Behind the Words."]] This exercise will help students critically analyze job advertisements and understand the importance of clear and precise language in job postings.

These sample ads are designed to reflect common language used in job postings and will provide a practical basis for discussion and analysis in your lesson.
