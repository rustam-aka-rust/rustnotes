## When AI Starts Communicating with Another AI, They Develop a Language

> [!shell]- **A Snail**:
> 
> - **IF** an AI agent gains the capability for autonomous problem-solving and decision-making,
> - **THEN** it will seek to communicate with other AI agents to enhance efficiency (develop common language),
> - **BECAUSE** communication fosters collaboration, and shared understanding often leads to the pursuit of mutual objectives.
 
- What if AI could learn from another AI? Isn't that precisely what we desire most—to alleviate the burden of routine labor? Intellectual work is increasingly mirroring physical labor: tedious, exhausting, and often devoid of immediate results, making it relentlessly difficult.
    - Yes, this is exactly what we seek, but are we prepared to pay the price? The moment an AI agent begins interacting with another AI agent, they inevitably construct a language comprehensible to both of them—but not necessarily to humans.
    - Once this shared language is established, the next logical progression is the pursuit of [[Машина и цель|a common objective]]. And this is where we verge into the realm of science fiction, where mere sequences of zeros and ones transcend their function and edge toward sentience.
